English

DynoCam is a video reframing and person-tracking app for iPhone and iPad.

Videos and photos

Videos you select are analyzed on your device. DynoCam does not upload your source or exported videos to a DynoCam server. An exported video is added to Photos only when you choose to save it.

Analytics, advertising, consent, and purchases

DynoCam uses Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, and Google User Messaging Platform. Depending on your consent, device settings, and region, these services may process app activity, device identifiers, diagnostics, advertising data, and consent choices. Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. DynoCam does not receive your full payment-card details.

Your choices

You can manage tracking and photo access in iOS Settings, make consent choices when prompted, or delete the app. For privacy requests, contact della.kimko@gmail.com.

Children and changes

DynoCam is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age in their region. We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change and will post the revised date here.
