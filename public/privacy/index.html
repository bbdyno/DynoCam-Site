English

DynoCam is a video reframing and person-tracking app for iPhone and iPad.

Videos and photos

Videos you select are analyzed on your device. DynoCam does not upload your source or exported videos to a DynoCam server. An exported video is added to Photos only when you choose to save it.

ML processing metrics and purchases

DynoCam uses Google ML Kit for on-device pose detection. ML Kit may send non-content metrics such as a per-installation identifier, app and device information, feature events, performance measurements, and error codes to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. Your video frames and pose results remain on device. Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions; DynoCam does not receive your full payment-card details.

Your choices

You can manage photo access in iOS Settings or delete the app. For privacy requests, contact della.kimko@gmail.com.

Children and changes

DynoCam is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age in their region. We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change and will post the revised date here.

選択とお問い合わせ

iOS設定で写真へのアクセスを管理できます。プライバシーに関するお問い合わせはdella.kimko@gmail.comまでご連絡ください。
